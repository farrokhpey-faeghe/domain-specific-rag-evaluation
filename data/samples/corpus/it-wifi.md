# Connecting to campus Wi-Fi

Students and staff should connect to **NU-Secure** and sign in with their NetID and password. NU-Secure is encrypted and works in every campus building.

Visitors can join **NU-Guest**. Guest access lasts 24 hours and needs a phone number to receive a one-time code.

Visiting students and researchers from other universities can use **eduroam** with their home institution's credentials.

Gaming consoles and smart TVs in residence halls must be registered at devices.northgate.example before they can connect.
