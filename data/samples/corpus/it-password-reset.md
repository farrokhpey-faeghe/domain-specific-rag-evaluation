# Resetting your NetID password

Go to password.northgate.example and choose **Forgot password**. You need your Duo two-factor device to finish the reset.

- Reset links expire after 15 minutes. If your link expired, start the reset again.
- New passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- After 10 failed sign-in attempts your account locks; the lock clears automatically after 30 minutes.
