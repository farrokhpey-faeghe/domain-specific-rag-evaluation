# Main Library hours

Regular semester hours:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | 7am to midnight |
| Friday | 7am to 8pm |
| Saturday | 10am to 6pm |
| Sunday | noon to midnight |

During finals week the Main Library is open 24 hours. Hours change during breaks, so check the calendar on the library website.
