# Ordering official transcripts

Order official transcripts through the **student portal** under *Records > Transcripts*.

- Electronic transcripts cost $10 each and usually arrive within 2 business days.
- Paper transcripts cost $15 each and are mailed within 5 business days.
- Unofficial transcripts are free and can be downloaded from the student portal at any time.

You cannot order a transcript while your account has a financial hold. Contact the Bursar's Office to clear a hold.
