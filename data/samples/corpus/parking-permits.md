# Student parking permits

Buy a student parking permit online through the **parking portal** at parking.northgate.example.

- A semester permit costs $180, and a full-year permit costs $320.
- Commuter permits are valid in Lots C and D.
- Overnight parking requires a residential permit, which is only available to students living on campus.

Lost your permit? Report it in the parking portal. Replacements cost $25.
