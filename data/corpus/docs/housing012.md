## Mccomas Village

McComas Village is made up of town-house style buildings that house seven sororities and seven fraternities. Each house contains formal living and dining areas, a full kitchen, laundry room, single rooms, two and three person rooms, and community bathrooms on living floors. Residents are generally members of a Greek chapter and are not required to purchase a meal plan.

### Mccomas Village Amenities

- Air conditioning
- Full kitchen
- Formal living and dining areas
- Finished basement
- Coin-free laundry
- Secure Access
- Closest to the Glass Bowl and Recreation Center

### Room Specifications

- Bed – XL twin, adjustable low loft
- Closet or wardrobe
- Dresser
- Desk
- Desk chair
- Window blinds
- Ceiling lights
