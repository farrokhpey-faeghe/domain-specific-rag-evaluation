## Students

Welcome to the University of Toledo! We are excited that you’ve chosen to be a Rocket. UToledo is committed to having a diverse, inclusive campus culture where everyone feels included and respected. Your identity and disability are part of the diverse fabric of our campus. As a team, we invite you to watch this short video as a way of letting you know that You Are a Rocket and You Belong!

If you are thinking about attending UToledo or are a current student interested in
learning more about how our office can support you, we invite you to schedule a prospective
student appointment. Once a student has been fully admitted to UToledo, they can initiate
affiliation to discuss access and set up accommodations.

## AFFILIATION: **CONNECTING IS AS EASY AS 1, 2, 3!**

1. Complete our online New Student Application
2. Submit documentation from your high school, another institution of higher education,
and/or health care provider or other professionals, which verifies medical condition(s)
and explains disability barriers to the educational experience

- Although many different types of disability related documentation may be acceptable, for your convenience you may ask your health care provider to complete our disability verification form.
- Please see ADR Documentation Guidelines for additional information.
- An email will be sent to your Rockets account once information you submit has been reviewed so please check your Rockets email regularly.
3. Since your input is critical, you must complete a disability access initial appointment with an ADR Accessibility Specialist to set up accommodations and receive training on the procedures for utilizing the accommodations. Once accommodations have been established, you will select the appropriate accommodation for each class for which you are enrolled through ADR’s online system.

Accommodations are typically not retroactive. To utilize accommodations, you must make formal requests in our Disability Accommodations Portal (DAP) system in order for instructors to be properly notified. The University of Toledo Office Of Accessibility and Disability Resources will also consider the unique characteristics of the course, program, or technical standards in order to determine whether an accommodation is reasonable.
