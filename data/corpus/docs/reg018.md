## Legal Name Change

To request a name change of your student record on Main Campus, present your social
security card along with the completed Name Change Application reflecting the new name to Rocket Solution Central, Rocket Hall Suite 1200. Phone:
419.530.8700; FAX: 419.530.4828.  Health Science Campus students may submit this information
to the Student Service Center, Mulford Library, First Floor.  Phone: 419.383.3600;
FAX: 419.383.3327. Please note that you may submit your Name Change Application with
the appropriate documentation either in person or by fax. **Your legal name must match the name indicated on the social security card.  No other
form of identification can be accepted.**

Students who are also employed by The University of Toledo should also present a new social security card as legal documentation reflecting the change to:

Human Resources Office

Rocket Hall 1200

1625 W. Rocket Drive

Toledo, Ohio 43606

Phone 419.530.4747 (HRHR)

## Legal Gender designation change

Students can change the legal gender designation on file with the University by providing proper documentation. The request should include a written statement from the student requesting the change (with the student's signature and date); supporting documentation should include ONE of the following: Driver's License, or State I.D., or Court paperwork that includes the gender designation.

The request and supporting documentation can be submitted to:

Rocket Solution Central

Rocket Hall

Suite 1200

Phone: 419.530.8700

FAX: 419.530.4828
