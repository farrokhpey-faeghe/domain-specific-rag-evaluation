## Registration Dates

We are in the open registration period for Spring 2027. Registration is open for all graduate students and all continuing undergraduate students.

New undergraduate and transfer students must complete orientation before registering for classes.

#### **Program 60**

**Spring 2027**Jan. 15, 2027

For more information on how to register, view our Step-by-Step Guide to Registration or Registration Frequently Asked Questions.

Registration opens for **all students** Feb. 17, 2027 at 8:00 a.m. There is no priority registration for summer.

New undergraduate and transfer students must complete orientation before registering for classes.

For more information on how to register, view our Step-by-Step Guide to Registration or Registration Frequently Asked Questions.

You can register for the entire year ― fall and spring semester ― at the same time.

**Registration will begin at 8:00 a.m. on each of the days listed below.**
