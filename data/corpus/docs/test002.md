#### Make-up Testing

Make-up Testing services are open to UToledo faculty in the event that a student is unable to complete an exam during regularly scheduled classroom time. \*This is only to be used in the case of excused absence according to the UToledo missed class policy.

#### Placement Testing

Placement tests are used to determine specific knowledge or proficiency in various subjects. A better placement test score can save time and money and help students advance to the next level in their academic journey.

#### Disability Accommodations

Faculty may arrange for students with testing accommodations to complete their tests at the Testing Center during operating hours. Ear plugs, noise canceling headsets or white noise machines are available, as are a limited number of assistive devices.

#### Outside Services

The University of Toledo Main Campus Testing Center is only available to UToledo students, faculty, and staff and does not offer outside proctoring services. Note: CLEP and DSST are also not being offered in the Testing Center at this time, as both now offer remote proctoring.

The Main Campus Testing Center is nationally certified through the National College Testing Association (NCTA).

The Main Campus Testing Center is closed for the summer as of Friday, May 15 and will reopen Monday, August 24. The fall semester begins Monday, August 31, 2026, at which time regular hours will resume.
