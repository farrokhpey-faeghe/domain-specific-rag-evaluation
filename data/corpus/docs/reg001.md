The Office of the Registrar is the custodian of your academic records.

- Oversees the registration process for classes (including add/drop)
- Determines graduation eligibility, prepares diplomas and plans commencement
- Documents whether prerequisites have been met
- Processes grades
- Prepares and sends student transcripts
