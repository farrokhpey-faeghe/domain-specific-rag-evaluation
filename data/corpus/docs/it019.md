## Connecting to the Virtual Lab

In order to access the UToledo Virtual Lab environment, you will need to download and install the VMware Horizon Client. Our Frequently Asked Questions page has some quick instructions on how to install and configure the VMWare Horizon Client for your computer.

Once you have the VMWare Horizon client installed, click on one of the locations below and log in with your UTAD credentials.

- vlab.utoledo.edu for academic virtual labs (like UT Open)
- vlabcl.utoledo.edu for clinical virtual labs (like Clinical Live)
