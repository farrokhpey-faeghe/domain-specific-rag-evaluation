#### Living Guide

Please review the Office of Residence Life Living Guide prior to coming to campus. The Living Guide includes residence hall policies and procedures, community expectations and a prohibited items list.

#### Packages and Mail

Student packages will be delivered to the Office of Residence Life and mail will be sent directly to the student residence hall. Packages and mailing addresses can be found on the addresses page.

#### Furnishings and Lofts

The furnishings in the residence halls for each student are:

- XL twin bed and mattress (measures 36" by 80")
- Desk and chair
- Chest of drawers (in some residence halls, two students share a chest of drawers)
- Wardrobe or closet space

#### MicroFridge Rentals

MicroFridge® is a convenient, all-in-one appliance that combines a microwave, refrigerator, and freezer, making it an ideal choice for any room or suite.

To make move-in easier, your MicroFridge® will be delivered directly to your room before you arrive. At the end of the academic year, it will be picked up for you after move-out. No heavy lifting required.

Units must be rented or purchased online or by calling 1-800-525-7307.
