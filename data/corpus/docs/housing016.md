## What to Bring to Campus

When beginning to pack for your move to UToledo, make sure to talk with your roommate and/or suitemates about items that you can share so you don't end up with two of the same large items.

### What to Bring to Campus (Permitted Items)

#### Kitchen Supplies

- Can Opener
- Cups/Glasses
- Plates
- Utensils

#### Laundry Supplies

- Iron and ironing board
- Laundry detergent
- Fabric softener

Access to laundry facilities included in your semester rate.

#### Personal Products

- Soap
- Toiletries
- Hangers
- Hair Dryer

Bring toilet paper if your room is suite style.

#### Linen

- Sheets and bedding (extra-long twin size)
- Towels and washcloths

#### Appliances and Electronics

- Refrigerator—4 cu. ft. or less - Rent a MicroFridge!
- Microwave—1000 watts or less
- Fan
- Coffee maker
- TV and desired streaming devices (record serial numbers)

#### Miscellaneous

- Cleaning supplies
- Surge protector and extension cords
- Paper towels
- Playing cards and games
- Throw rugs or carpet
- Tissues

### What to Leave at Home (Prohibited Items)

- Sandwich Makers
- Waffle Irons
- George Foreman Type Grills
- Hot Plates
- Electric Griddles and/or Electric Skillets
- Air Fryers
- Instant Pots and/or Pressure Cookers
- Rice Cookers
- Coffee Makers without Automatic Shutoff
- Food Dehydrators
- Space Heaters
- Electric Blankets
- Adaptors and Extension Cords without Surge Protection
- Amplified Sound Equipment
- Air Conditioner
- Incense
- Candles
- Candle Warmers and Wax Melters
- Toaster Oven
- Toaster
- Incense
- Drug Paraphernalia
- Hover-boards
- Adhesive LED Strip Lights
- Neon Lights
- Lithium Batteries
- Weapons (Real, Fake, Toy, Look Real)
- Sun Lamps/Halogen Lamps/Lava Lamps
- 3D printers
