## Rocket Card

The Rocket Card is your official identification card for The University of Toledo. You'll use it all over campus to identify yourself as a member of the University community. The Rocket Card has many uses, from building access to meal plan purchases! Explore this website for additional information. The University of Toledo is thrilled to announce Rocket Mobile ID, your digital way of accessing everything your physical Rocket Card does and more!

### See Rocket Mobile ID in Action

Rocket Mobile ID gives current UToledo students and employees a convenient digital version of their Rocket Card on an eligible mobile device.

Watch this quick video to see how Rocket Mobile ID works and how to get started.

### What can I do with my rocket card/Rocket Mobile ID?

**Identification Card**

The Rocket Card and Rocket Mobile ID now serve as your official University of Toledo
identification.

Use either form to redeem Meal Swipes and Dining Dollars.

Shuttle Services

Ride any Rocket Lift or TARPS (reservation needed) bus for free when you show either
identification.

Financial Aid

Load excess financial aid to your Rocket Card account to purchase books and supplies.

Use your physical ID or cellphone as your key to enter your residence hall, labs,
study areas and more.

Enter any UToledo sporting event for free when you show your Rocket Card or Rocket
Mobile ID.

UToledo Discounts

**Library** Card

Check out books and digital resources using  either form of identification.

To learn more, visit our frequently asked questions page.
