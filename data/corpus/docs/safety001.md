##### Police

The UToledo Police Department provides 24-hour uniformed police services to all three UToledo campuses serving a daily population of more than 31,000 students, faculty, staff, patients, and visitors. Learn more

##### Hospital Security

The UToledo Health Science Campus includes over fifteen uniformed Security Officers who conduct 24-hour security services to the University Medical Center and assist staff, patients and visitors. Learn more

##### Emergency Preparedness

This site discusses the appropriate responses to a wide range of situations, including fire, evacuation, weather-related emergencies, and other situations that may impact the operations of the University. Learn more

##### Environmental Health & Safety

The mission of the Environmental Health and Radiation Safety Department is to develop, implement and monitor programs of environmental and occupational safety and health. Learn more

##### UToledo Alert

UToledo’s safety notification system, which includes text alerts and email notifications of emergency situations on campus. This service is free to students, faculty and staff. Learn more
