## Lift Shuttle

Groome Transportation is The University of Toledo's on-campus shuttle service operator. Together, our goal is to enhance convenience and safety for the University's current student population, as well as our active faculty and staff. Please carry your Rocket Card or have your Rocket Mobile ID ready for verification purposes when asked by our Lift drivers.

During spring and fall semesters only, shuttle services will continuously run the Blue Loop on Main Campus and split off for runs to the Center for the Visual Arts at the Toledo Museum of Art and Health Science Campus at scheduled times.

The vehicles will be ADA compliant to provide accessible entrance and exit for riders with mobility issues and feature security cameras for audio-video surveillance.

### REAL-TIME lift TRACKING

You can download the TransLoc app from Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. The TransLoc app allows you to see in real time where a shuttle is on a specific route and when it will be arriving at your stop. Up-to the minute announcements regarding situations that impact the arrival time including inclement weather, are viewable.

*NOTE:To download the free app, search for and install TransLoc app and select The
University of Toledo.*

### blue Loop

- The Blue Loop has 2 shuttles operating continuously to main campus that begin and end at the same point.
- Monday-Thursday 7:32am – 10:32pm
- Friday 7:32am to 7:32pm
- A new shuttle passes a stop within Blue Loop every 16-20 minutes, meaning that if you just missed one shuttle, you may have a short wait for the next to come.

### Lost/Found ITems

Items left on or found on the shuttles will be taken to the Student Union Administrative Offices, room 2525A-E.

ID/Rocket Card left on or found on the shuttles will be take to The Student Union, Rocket Card Office, Room 2525.

Riders will need to have a reservation, present a valid UToledo ID  and be TARTA Move
