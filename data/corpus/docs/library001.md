Mulford Library hours are extended during M2 Dedicated Study, and during the week before finals week and finals week for fall and spring semesters. Changes in hours due to holidays, breaks, and emergencies are posted in the library and on the Mulford Facebook page; see the complete list of Mulford Library's hours of operation.

Alternate study spaces available after the library closes:

- First floor of the Mulford Library Building annex (the lounge area, open 24/7)
- Mulford Annex Treehouse (third-floor lounge, swipe access, open 24/7)
- Lounge/computer lab near HEB 103/105
- Lounge area on the first floor of the Wolfe Center
- Hospital cafeteria dining room
- Mulford Cafe (Mulford Basement)
