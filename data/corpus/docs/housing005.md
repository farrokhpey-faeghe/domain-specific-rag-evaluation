## Greek Village Housing Application

- The Greek student housing applications for the 2026-27 academic year is now available through your myUT portal.
- Please note that the online housing application is the only one that we offer. If you do not have access to a computer or need assistance with your application, please contact the Office of Residence Life at 419.530.5214 or reslife@utoledo.edu.
- If you are new member to your organization, please contact your chapter's house manager and the Office of Fraternity and Sorority Life to confirm your status has been updated in order to ensure the Greek student housing application is made available to you.

### **THE APPLICATION PROCESS**

#### **Step 1: Apply**

- Access the University Housing application via the myUT portal by selecting "University Housing Application" under the Student tab.
- Select the term "Fall 2026" (the housing contract is for the 2026-27 academic year, including both fall and spring semester).

#### Step 2: Room Assignment

- Meet with your chapter's house manager to discuss your roommates and room assignment.
- You will be able to select your specific room during your room selection timeslot.

### Frequently asked questions

**Application**

**What if I want to cancel prior to the semester starting?**If you are a returning student who is not planning to return, or if you are not required
to live on campus and choose to cancel your housing contract, email reslife@utoledo with your request. When you email, please include your Rocket Number and explain your
situation.

**McComas Village residents**must also complete the Greek signature page located on the housing forms page (McComas Village Supplemental Contract Release).

**What if I want to cancel during the semester?**You will need to submit a contract cancellation request.

**McComas Village residents**must also complete the Greek signature page located on the housing forms page (McComas Village Supplemental Contract Release).

**How do I know my application is complete?**There are multiple ways to check to ensure your application is complete.

#### **Fees**

**What is the cost of housing for 2026-27?**Housing fees are billed in July for the fall semester. View the housing rates.

**Can I mail in my payment?**No. All payments must be received online via the myUT Portal. If you cannot make payment
online, contact the Office of Residence Life at 419.530.2941 or reslife@utoledo.edu.

**Can my financial aid cover the housing application and pre-payment (deposit) fee?**No. In order to secure a housing assignment, we need the payment at the time of the
application. If you are on a full-ride scholarship or cannot make payment online,
contact the Office of Residence Life at 419.530.2941 or reslife@utoledo.edu.

**What should I do if I cannot make the payment now?**All fees are required at the time of application. If you cannot make payment online,
contact the Office of Residence Life at 419.530.2941 or reslife@utoledo.edu.

#### **Meal Plan**

**Do I have to be on a meal plan?**No, if you live in McComas Village, it is optional to have a meal plan. Check out
the Meal Plan page for meal plan options.
