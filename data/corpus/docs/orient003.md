## 100% Online Degree Students

### Online Orientation for online students

Before registering for classes, all online students are encouraged to complete their online orientation through their myUT portal. Orientation will introduce you to resources available to you as an online student and prepare you for success as a new student!

Once you're ready to register for classes, contact Academic Advising at 419.530.4444 or by email at advising@utoledo.edu.

### HOW TO ACCESS ORIENTATION

To access orientation, follow these steps:

1. Under the New Online Student tab, select step two, Complete Orientation Online.

1. Complete all of the modules - you will know you are done when the top of the screen says "You've Completed Online Orientation."

### Do you want to complete an on-campus degree instead?

Contact Admissions at 419.530.8888 if you need to be updated to an on-campus degree program. You would then complete orientation for on campus students for your appropriate start term and student type. Click here for on campus student orientation details and select your admitted term for more information.
