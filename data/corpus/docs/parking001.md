## Parking Services

**Our mission is to provide superior and quality services through leadership, innovation
and stewardship.**

Our purpose is to provide students, employees, affiliates and visitors the ability to obtain parking permits and accessible spaces and to apprise the university community of events that could affect parking on campus. Visit ParkUToledo to learn more.

The University of Toledo selected Groome Transportation as its new on-campus shuttle service operator to enhance convenience and safety for students, faculty and staff. Visit the shuttle transportation webpage for more information.

Download the VEO app! Stand up, sit down or pedal bike to effortlessly move around campus! Vist the VEO scooters webpage for more information.

Visit the campus directions webpage to learn how to navigate your way around our campuses.

Ohio's city-to-city bus service, with daily bus schedules 365-days-a-year. UToledo bus stop is located at 1533 S Towerview Blvd. All UToledo students and faculty: use coupon code GOROCKETS for 50% off all GoBus tickets during the 2026-27 academic year. Visit ridegobus.com for schedule information.
