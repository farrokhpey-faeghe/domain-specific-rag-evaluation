## 2026 - 2027 Housing and Meal Plans

All students who live in the residence halls are required to be on a meal plan except students who live in McComas Village. Meal plans are prepaid each semester
as part of the housing fee and offers UT students the assurance of being able to eat
without having to carry cash.

For additional information and explanation of meal plans, visit the Dining Services website.

For additional information on living on campus, visit the Residence Life website.
