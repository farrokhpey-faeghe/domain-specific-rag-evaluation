### Practices:

- **According to the policy, a 15 week course must meet all 15 weeks-including exam week.**
- **Exams are held in the same classroom as the course during the assigned exam time,
except when notified by the Registrar's Office.**
- **If a course does not fall under one of the listed times, please reach out and the
Registrar's Office will assign an exam time.**
- Class starting on the quarter, half hour or other, use the on-hour start time. For example, class that begin at 8:15, 8:30 or 8:45 use the exam schedule for 8:00.
- Classes that meet only one day per week will usually have exam scheduled that same day. Confirm with instructor.
- Parts of Term 8 and 9 will have exams on the last day of class.
- Classes which meet on Saturdays or during the Summer term will hold exams during the last day of class.
- For instructors teaching online courses: If you need to offer an on-campus exam in a general purpose room, please email curriculum-scheduling@utoledo.edu to reserve a time and room.
- All exam schedules and classrooms can be located in Astra by the academic department.
