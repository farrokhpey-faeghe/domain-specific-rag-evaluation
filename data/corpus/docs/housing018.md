## University Housing Contract Cancellation

#### Housing Cancellation Request

**Once occupancy is taken (key received by student or items moved into assigned space)
cancellation is not guaranteed.** Any student wishing to cancel their housing contract should submit a Housing Cancellation
Request. You will receive an email within 3 business days with the status of your
request.

**Students are encouraged to refrain from entering into another living arrangement/agreement
or lease until they receive a decision releasing them from their Housing Contract.**

Begin your Contract Cancellation request here.

#### Housing Refund Policy

Refunds will follow the University's Percentage Refund Dates for 15-week semester based on the date the student returns their keys to the Office of Residence Life and their room is vacated and prepared for the next resident.

#### Post Occupancy

After taking occupancy of the room (key received by student or items moved into assigned space) cancellation of this contract will not be considered due to dietary or other special needs other than those listed below. Cancellation of this contract will be considered only for current residents with the required documentation under the following conditions:

| **Reason for Cancellation** | **Documentation Required** |
| Academic Suspension or Dismissal | Nothing Required. Housing will verify. |
| Withdrawal | Nothing Required. Housing will verify. |
| Graduation | Nothing Required. Housing will verify. |
| Co-Op | Official co-op offer letter. |
| Marriage | Student must upload dated copy of Marriage Certificate; Date must be within Housing Contract dates. |
| Military- Activation of Unit/Required Training | Student must upload a copy of military orders on official letterhead confirming duty or training dates and location. |
| Participating in Study Abroad/Exchange Program | Student must upload a copy of letter or email from university official confirming participation in program. |
| Student has Dependent Child(ren) | Student must upload copy of most recent federal tax return verifying dependent child; if none is available, student must upload documentation verifying they are the parent of a child living with them (see proof of residency documents for minor). |
| Financial | Change in financial situation that has occurred within the housing contract dates. FAFSA completed for domestic students; verifiable financial documents for both domestic and international students. See specific information below. |
| Transferring to Another Institution | Nothing Required. Housing will verify. |
| Greek Office Term Expired | Only applicable to offices that expire at end of Fall Semester and student must have completed their live-on requirement. |

**Financial Changes**

Students may request a contract cancellation based on a significant change in their
financial situation **during the housing contract dates**. Verifiable documentation is required for consideration. Examples include:

- Parental job loss or reduction in income (employer letter, termination notice, or pay stubs)

- Death of a parent/guardian or immediate family member who provides financial support (obituary or death certificate)

- Unexpected medical expenses for the student or an immediate family member (medical bills or insurance statements)

- Divorce or separation of parents that impacts household income (legal documentation)

- Other significant verified changes in household financial circumstances (e.g., foreclosure, eviction, natural disaster expenses)
