## Mulford Hours of Operation

| **The Mulford Library is on regular hours**. Alternative study locations on HSC are listed on the Insider's Guide to Mulford and HSC |

- Regular Hours
- Exam Week Hours: Mulford is open for extended hours during M2 Dedicated Study, and during the week before and week of finals for fall and spring semesters.
- Holiday Hours

After 7:00 pm, access to the Mulford Library Building is restricted to UToledo students, faculty, and staff. Use your access/proxy card to get into the building.

| Friday - Saturday | 7:30 am - 9:00 pm |
