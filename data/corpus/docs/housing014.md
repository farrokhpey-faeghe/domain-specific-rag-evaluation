## Parks Tower

Offering traditional corridor style living, this is UToledo's only all first-year residence hall. Traditional style bedrooms include two-person rooms with community bathrooms. Parks Tower was renovated in 2018 with kitchens, lounges, study spaces, a new computer lab, updated lobby, and brand-new furniture throughout the building.

### Parks Tower Amenities

- Air conditioning
- Shared kitchens
- Shared study and lounge spaces
- Single rooms available
- Community bathrooms
- Coin-free laundry
- Work out room
- Computer lab
- Secure Access
- 24-hour front desk
- Vending machines
- Co-ed by wing
- Outdoor bike storage
- Closest to the Glass Bowl and the Flatlands

### Room Specifications

- Bed – XL twin, adjustable loft with convenient side table
- Built-in closet and shelves
- Shared dresser
- Desk with overhead carrel with power, lights, and dry erase surface
- Desk chair
- Window shades
- Ceiling lights

### Holistic Wellness Living Learning Community

The Holistic Wellness Living Learning Community (HWLLC), located in Parks Tower, is an opportunity open to all students committed to living life well-balanced. As a member of the HWLLC, you will enhance your knowledge of and create your own definition of personal wellness. The community is nurtured through interactive programming, and interaction with students, faculty and staff members. Engaging, fun team building programs with the support of a HWLLC mentor will help you focus on positive health behavior change.
