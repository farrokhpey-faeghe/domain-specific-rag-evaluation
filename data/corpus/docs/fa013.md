## Your Financial Aid How-To Guide

Whether you’re a new or continuing graduate student, figuring out how to pay for graduate
school can be overwhelming. But that’s why we’re here — to help take away some of
the anxiety.

Check out these clear, simple steps for applying for financial aid and navigating
the process. And remember, we’re just a phone call or email away if you have questions.

If you’re an international graduate student, visit our pages just for you.

Accept Aid

You’ve done all the hard work. Now it’s time to see how much aid you qualify for,

Your Summer To-Do List

Take a breath. Review our checklist to make sure you’ve done everything you need to
do regarding your financial aid before you come to campus.
