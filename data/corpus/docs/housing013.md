## Ottawa house east and West

Ottawa House consists of two buildings – Ottawa East and Ottawa West. This is a suite-style residence hall with four person suites with two double bedrooms and limited five person suites with one triple bedroom and two single bedrooms. Each suite includes a furnished living room and private bathroom. Students living in Ottawa House have access to both Ottawa East and West. The Food Hall at Ottawa East is steps away from both buildings.

### Ottawa East and West Amenities

- Air conditioning
- Private bathroom within suite
- Shared kitchens
- Shared study and lounge spaces
- Coin-free laundry
- Work out room
- Computer lab – Ottawa East
- Secure Access
- Dining hall located within Ottawa East
- Vending machines
- Co-ed throughout the building
- Outdoor bike storage
- Closest to Rocket Hall, Gateway and the Food Hall at Ottawa East

### Room Specifications

- Bed – XL twin, adjustable low loft
- Closet or wardrobe
- Dresser
- Desk
- Desk chair
- Couches and chairs
- Window blinds
- Ceiling lights

### Arts Living Learning Community

The Arts Living Learning Community (ALLC) is designed for students majoring in or interested in the arts. Community members are encouraged to explore their creative voices and see how all branches of the arts–visual, musical, acting, creative writing and film–are connected. The community offers residents an artistically-enhanced lifestyle with high impact programming, social events and access to an art studio on your floor, library, piano and computer lab located in Ottawa East.
