### Care Where You Are

Welcome to the University Counseling Center, offering confidential services to currently enrolled students. We are now a clinic of UToledo Health, serving students from Main and Health Science campuses. Services include individual and group therapy with in-person and telehealth options. We provide initial services at no additional cost to currently enrolled UToledo students. Long term services are provided through insurance or private pay options. Schedule your first appointment to meet with one of our licensed providers and learn more.

On-demand crisis care and general support is available to all students 24/7 by calling 419.530.2273.

We also encourage all students to download the new Mantra Health app or visit Mantra’s website to connect with self-help tools, video support that is available 8 a.m.–midnight or the peer-to-peer platform. Use your University student email to create a free account.

For crisis care, know who to call and where to go.

Students can call or text 988 for the National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, call 911 for an immediate emergency or utilize the University’s 24/7 crisis support line by calling 419.530.2273.

In-person crisis support is available from 9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 3 p.m. at the University Counseling Center. We encourage those looking for in-person crisis support to call 419.530.2426 prior to arrival when possible.

Students can also visit the Mantra app or website to connect with virtual services.
