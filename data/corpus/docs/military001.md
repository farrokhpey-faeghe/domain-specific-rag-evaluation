Our Military Service Center recognizes the sacrifices of our service members and their
families and is dedicated to helping them achieve continued success. This will be
accomplished by providing accessible education, degree completion opportunities, and
customized support services.

The Military Service Center has moved to the new Welcome Center in Tucker Hall 0204.
