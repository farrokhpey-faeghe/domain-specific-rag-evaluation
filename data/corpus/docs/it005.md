## DESKTOP SUPPORT

**IT Desktop Support**is responsible for providing primary support for the University of Toledo Main and Health Science campus, by serving as a single point of contact for end-user hardware and software needs. IT Desktop Support is also responsible for providing a variety of configuring, installing, and supporting end-user computing solutions. (i.e. hardware, software, printers)
