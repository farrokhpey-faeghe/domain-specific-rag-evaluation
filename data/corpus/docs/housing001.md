## Welcome Home

Experience the vibrant energy of campus life at The University of Toledo — where living on campus isn’t just convenient, it’s a core part of the Rocket experience.

Each residence hall at UToledo has its own personality, just like the students who call it home. Whether you're looking for connection, convenience, or a place that feels like yours, you’ll find it here.

Living on campus gives students the opportunity to build lifelong friendships, get involved, and stay connected to everything happening at UToledo. Research shows that students who live on campus tend to earn higher GPAs and graduate on time more often than their off-campus peers. Because of the strong connection between housing and student success, UToledo requires first- and second-year students to live on campus.

## The Power To Do Big Things in Your Own Space

Living on campus means more than just having a place to stay — it’s your gateway to community, connection, and success. Whether you’re a new student exploring your options, a returning Rocket looking for your next space, or simply curious about the benefits and amenities of campus living, we’re here to support you every step of the way.

## Life on Campus

Living at UToledo means more than just a place to sleep — it’s where memories are made. Dive into a vibrant community filled with friendships, events, and everyday campus experiences.
