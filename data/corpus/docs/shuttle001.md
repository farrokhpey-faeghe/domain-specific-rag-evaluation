## TransLoc shuttle Stops

Lift drivers are permitted to pick up and drop of riders only at the following stops:

1\. Transportation Center (south side of Main Campus)

2\. Lofts/Barnes & Noble

3\. Ottawa House

4\. Center for Performing Arts

5\. Honors Academic Village (south side of building near Lot 13)

6\. Lancelot Thompson Student Union (bus loop near Field House)

7\. University Hall (Bancroft) 1

8\. University Hall (Bancroft) 2

9\. Old Towne

10\. North Engineering (NE)

11\. Health & Human Services (near Lot 11)

12\. Student Recreation Center (near Glass Bowl)

13\. Toledo Museum of Art

14\. UTMC Health Education
