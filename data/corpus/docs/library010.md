## Carlson Library Reservation Information

Carlson Library has open conference rooms and classrooms available to be reserved by University of Toledo faculty, staff, or student organizations.

**Student Organizations must use InvoNet to secure reservations to CL 2003 – Student Organization Suite. Individual Students
must use the Room Reservation Form to secure reservations to CL 1024K – Student Interview Room. At this time, these
are the only spaces that may be booked by students.**

Students may not use their personal contact information to make requests on behalf of a Faculty/Staff member for reservations in Carlson Library.

Patrons must have a valid library record in good standing to reserve a room (i.e. no excessive fines or blocks). Users must also follow the library's policies:

- Please be respectful of others and be mindful of your noise level.
- Please make sure all drinks have lids.
- Please clean up before you leave the room.
- Leaving personal property in the room will not "hold" the room. The library is not responsible for items left unattended.
- If policies are not followed, the library maintains the right to ask groups to leave.

Reservation Information

- Reservations must be submitted via online reservation request.
- Reoccurring reservations may be made for a singular semester (resubmission for additional semesters is required).
- Reservations for the current week can possibly be arranged from Monday – Friday until 5pm by contacting libraryreservations@utoledo.edu
- Same day reservations are not guaranteed if room is vacant.
- Must receive confirmation to accept reservation.
- Drop-in reservations cannot be accommodated at this time.

## Conference & Classroom Space Details

### CL1005 - Main Event Space

- Available for reservation to University of Toledo faculty and staff
- Priority given to University Libraries faculty and staff
- Can accommodate up to:

- 120 lecture style seating,
- 48 to 72 with round tables,
- 96 with long tables seated face to face
- 48 with long tables facing front for a presentation
- Wireless presentation technology, integrated audio, and dual projectors installed
- Lectern and microphones
- Seating/Furniture arrangement must be clarified when reserving

### CL1005B - Conference Room

- Available for reservation to University Libraries faculty and staff (special requests directed to libraryreservation@utoledo.edu)
- Can accommodate 8 at central table
- Monitor with wireless connection
- Kits including dry erase markers can be checked out at Circulation

### CL1024k - Student Interview Room

- Available for reservation by students only
- This room may not be used for group/individual work or study
- Can accommodate 1 student at a time
- Desk with workstation

### CL2003 - Student Organization Suite

- Available for reservation by student organizations only
- This room may not be used for group work or group study
- Can accommodate 16 around central table
- Reservations are limited to 3 hours per day per organization
- Large wall-mounted monitor with HDMI and VGA inputs and a laptop, mounted whiteboard
- Kits including dry erase markers can be checked out at Circulation

### CL2024 - Dorothy Price Model Classroom

- Available for reservation to University of Toledo faculty and staff
- Can accommodate 22 seated around rolling tables (fully customizable by attendees)
- Instructors station, laptop, mounted whiteboard
- Interactive monitor with wireless connection available
- Kits including dry erase markers can be checked out at Circulation

### CL3027 - Open Conference Room

- Available for reservation to University of Toledo faculty and staff
- Can accommodate 14 at central table
- Instructors station with laptop, projector, mounted whiteboard
- Kits including HDMI cables, clicker, & dry erase markers can be checked out at Circulation

### First-floor Tabling

- Available for reservation to University of Toledo faculty and staff
- Tabling is limited to the first floor
- Any food or drink must receive prior approval and only pre packaged items will be allowed. Please contact the event coordinator for the University Libraries
- Tabling will not be available when there are large events scheduled in the main event space
