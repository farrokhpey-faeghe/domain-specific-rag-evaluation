## Health Science Campus Office of Financial Aid

Welcome to The University of Toledo Health Science Campus. Financing your education is as important for most students as selecting the right school. Understanding the financial aid process and sources of funding available will help you as you begin to develop the financial plan necessary to meet your educational goals.

A Financial Aid staff member is available through the Student Service Center.

The Health Science Campus Office of Student Financial Aid is located on the 1st floor of Mulford Library. Office hours are 8:30 a.m. - 5:00 p.m. Monday - Friday.
