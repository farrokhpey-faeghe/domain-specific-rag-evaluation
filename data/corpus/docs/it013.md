## Legal Name Change

### **Name Change Request**

To request a name change of your student record, please visit this website.

#### **Current or Previous Student:**

- Location: Health Science Campus - Student Service Center, Mulford Library, First Floor
- Location: Main Campus - Rocket Solution Central, Rocket Hall Suite 1200 419.530.8700 / Registrar 419.530.4845

#### **Employees:**

- To change your name on your UTAD account, contact Human Resources at 419.530.4747

#### **Affiliates:**

- An Affiliate who has always been only an affiliate (never a student / never an employee) will
need their supervisor to update their name in the **affiliate system**
