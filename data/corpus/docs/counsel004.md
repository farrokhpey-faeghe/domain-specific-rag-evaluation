## Schedule an Appointment

## WHO IS ELIGIBLE TO USE COUNSELING CENTER SERVICES?

University of Toledo students who are:

- Regularly admitted\*
- Currently enrolled
- Located in Ohio (during time of service)

Out of the state- Students can access mental health services via Mantra Health if not located in Ohio.

For questions concerning eligibility, please call (419) 530-2426.

\*If you are an employee of the university please click here for information regarding services available to you.

\*\*In the event of a serious medical/mental health condition or family emergency, a student may request a withdrawal from classes. All inquiries about medical/mental health withdrawals should be directed to Rocket Solution Central at (419) 530-8700.

### SCHEDULE YOUR INITIAL APPOINTMENT

Click here to make your first appointment.\* You will complete your initial paperwork and select an appointment time, please allow 30 minutes to complete the scheduling process. If you have already made an appointment, you only need to complete the paperwork and do not need to select another appointment time. Your initial appointment will be scheduled as a video appointment via the WebEx platform. If you have questions please call our front desk at 419-530-2426.

Initial appointments are automatically scheduled as **virtual appointments**, your **video and audio** must be enabled for this appointment to occur. Phone/audio only appointments are
not available. If you prefer to attend your initial appointment in person, please
call the front desk at 419-530-2426 to request your appointment be changed to in-person.

*\*If you have questions or  if you are experiencing a mental health* crisis *please call 419-530-7827. Emergency counselors are available 24/7 via phone.*
