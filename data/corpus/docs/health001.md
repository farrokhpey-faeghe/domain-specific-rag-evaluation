## University of Toledo Health and Wellness Programs

The University of Toledo provides **comprehensive, integrated health and wellness services** to students, faculty and staff.

### Mental Health Resources

#### For Students

Students receive **free mental health services** at the Counseling Center on Main Campus.

#### For Employees

UToledo employees and their family members are offered **confidential mental health support** through the AllOne Health Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

### Main Campus - University Health Center
