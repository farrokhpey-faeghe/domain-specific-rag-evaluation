## Advanced Placement (AP) Credit

The University of Toledo will follow our normal process of applying credit and course equivalencies (if appropriate) to AP exams if the student provides results of the AP exam taken and receives the required score to award credit for a particular exam.

On recommendation of the departments concerned, full college credit may be granted for such work or certain prerequisite courses may be waived to permit the student to enroll in more advanced courses.

If your scores were not sent to UToledo after your original examination, please use the College Board website to request them.

The University of Toledo AP Code is **1845.**
