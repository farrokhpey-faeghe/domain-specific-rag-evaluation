**Getting Started with Teams**

Teams provides an intuitive, chat-based workspace that enables you to easily have conversations, host meetings, share files, and collaborate on documents in real time from any device.

For people new to Microsoft Teams, we suggest the interactive Teams Demonstration provided by Microsoft. The interactive Teams demo is available at https://teamsdemo.office.com/

Additional resources include the Teams Quick Start Guide (pdf) and Teams video training from Microsoft.

#### FAQ

**I don't want Teams to open automatically when I login to my computer, how can I fix
this?**

Click on your account image/initials in the upper right corner of Teams and choose
Settings.  De-select the **Auto-start** application checkbox.
