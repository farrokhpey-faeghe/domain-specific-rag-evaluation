## Office of the Treasurer

### Payment Due Dates

**Fall:**August 5 |

**Spring:**January 5 |

**Summer:**May 5

- Due dates represent the date that payment must be received and processed.
- Any new registration activity and/or charges after the published due date above will
be due daily.
- Select "Important Dates" tab below for more information.
**Note:** Some programs, such as medical programs, may have different payment due dates. Please refer to "Important Dates" for details.

The Treasurer’s Office is a unit of Finance and Administration that provides quality account management services to students, parents, and university departments. Functions of the department include:

- Oversee the collection of payments of University receipts and deposits by departments and student payments including third party payments, and 529 plan payments
- eCommerce/Merchant Services
- Student accounts management, including tuition and fee schedule, authorized users, billing, financial holds, and refunds
- Special Accounts, collections and accounting for student loans and past-due accounts
- Financial Wellness Counseling
- Processing of the IRS Tax Form 1098-T (Tuition Statement)
