## Passport Information and Photos

### Get a Passport

The Center of International Studies and Programs (CISP) can assist UToledo community members in obtaining a U.S. passport for an overseas learning experience. All travelers must have a valid passport to leave and reenter the United States.

U.S. students requiring a passport should not wait until the last minute; to order and process your passport can take 6-8 weeks. If traveling within 2 weeks, we suggest going through the U.S. Department of State.

For more information on processing times and getting a passport in a hurry, go to U.S. Department of State's Passports page.

The Center for International Studies and Programs is a Passport Acceptance Facility and accepts passport applications on behalf of the U.S. Department of State. You may pick up a passport application and get your passport photo taken right here on UToledo's Main Campus. For a map of campus, go to the UToledo directions page.

### Schedule an Appointment

To turn in your U.S. Passport Application at UToledo's Center for International Studies and Programs, an appointment is required. Call 419.530.5268 to schedule.

### Obtain a First-Time Passport

Below is a list of the things you will need to apply for a new passport.

- Passport Application (DS-11 form)
- Proof of Citizenship (birth certificate, proof of naturalization, consular report of birth abroad, or certificate of citizenship)
- Current Identification (driver's license or government issued identification card)
- Passport Photos are $5 for two photos, printed on site immediately after photo
- Passport Fee is $130 with an execution fee of $35; $60 fee to expedite the process. Fees are subject to change.

### Get your Photo

CISP can take your photo: $5 for two photos, printed on site immediately after your photo is taken.

### Payment Options

Passport fee: Check or money order (made payable to U.S. Department of State)

Photos and Execution fee: Pay with a credit card at UToledo Passport Services.

For up-to-date fees, refer to the U.S. Department of State website.

When you receive your new passport, make a photocopy of the page with your photo and signature and keep it separate from your passport. In the event of your passport being lost or stolen, having the information on this page handy will speed up the replacement process. Immediately report a lost or stolen passport to the U.S. consulate, and U.S. officials will direct you as to what to do.

### Resources

#### Passport Resources

We encourage you to visit the U.S. Department of State website which has complete passport information, applications, additional forms, and international travel information. Go to the DOS Passport website.

#### Passport Renewal

To renew your passport, check the specifics at the DOS Passport Renewal website.

#### Parking at UToledo

You will need a parking permit if you would like to park on campus. Cars parked on campus without a parking ticket WILL BE FINED. You can purchase either a daily or an hourly parking pass. You can learn more about it and purchase your parking ticket at ParkUToledo's site.
