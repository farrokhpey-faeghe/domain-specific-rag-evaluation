## Rocket Card FAQ's

**What is the Rocket Card (ID)?**

The Rocket Card is your official University of Toledo identification card. You will
use it all over campus to identify yourself as a member of the University community.
The Rocket Card has many uses, from building access to meal plan purchases!

**Who needs a Rocket Card (ID)?**

UToledo students, faculty, staff and affiliates are required to have a Rocket Card.

**How do I order my student Rocket Card (ID)?**

If you are a new student, you should order and pick-up your Rocket Card during Rocket
Launch, the new student orientation. If you did not receive your Rocket Card, please
follow the steps below:

- Log into your MyUT
- Click **Request New/Replacement Rocket Card**, which you will find under **My Other Resources**
- Review personal information
- Select Rocket Card (ID) pick-up location
- Upload your photo
- Click **Submit**

The Rocket Card Office will send a confirmation email to your UToledo account when your request has been successfully submitted. They will send an email to the same account when your ID is ready for pick-up.

**Where do I pick up my Rocket Card?**

When you order your Rocket Card (ID) you will be given the choice of two pick-up locations:

- **Main Campus:** Student Union room 2525
- **Health Science Campus:** Hospital Security, MLB Basement, room 007

In order to pick-up your Rocket Card (ID) you will need a valid picture ID.

**Can I use my preferred first name on my Rocket Card?**

Absolutely, if you decide to update your card to display your preferred first name, you can change it by following these steps:

- Log in to the myUT portal at myut.utoledo.edu
- Click on the **Student** tab
- Under **MY TOOLKIT**, in the **My Other Resources** section**,** click **Update Personal Information,** sign in and click on **Personal Information,** click Edit under **Personal Details**.
- Your default preferred first name is your legal first name. Enter your preferred first
name in the box and click the **Update** button

Once saved, your preferred first name will sync to various systems after 24 hours.

**Where can I find my card number? (To be used for Barnes and Noble online orders)**

- Visit myut.utoledo.edu
- In the top right corner click **Account Maintenance**
- Click the light blue box that says **Login to Manage Your Services** and log in with your UTAD credentials
- On the top yellow menu bar click **Your Account (Full)**
- You campus card number will be listed under **Personal Information**
- If your Rocket Card was just printed, it will take 24 hours to populate

**Understanding the Funds that can be found on my Rocket Card**

1.  **Dining Dollars:** Dining Dollars are a component of student meal plans that are loaded onto the Rocket
Card.  They are used for purchases at any of the Main Campus retail locations, dining
hall or convenience stores as well as Phoenicia Mediterranean Cuisine.
2. **Dining Dollars+:** Dining Dollars+ can be loaded onto the Rocket Card and are used for purchases in exchange
for carrying cash.  Dining Dollars+ are valid at all main campus dining locations *excluding* Phoenicia and 4 Seasons Bistro (at HSC).  To add Dining Dollars+ to your Rocket Card
today, visit https://dineoncampus.com/utoledo/meal-plandining-dollars--purchase.
3. **Dining Dollars+ Bonus:** Dining Dollars+ Bonus is a gift from Rocket Dining (amount subject to vary, typically
10%) when you load $100 or more in one transaction.  These funds are valid at the
same venues as Dining Dollars+ and are used for purchases after the Dining Dollars+
funds have been depleted.
4. **Phoenicia Dollars:**  Phoenicia Dollars are only valid at Phoenicia Cuisine, which is located on the 4th Floor of the Student Union.  To add Phoenicia Dollars to
your Rocket Card today, visit rocketcardplus.utoledo.edu.
5. **Birthday Rewards:** Birthday Rewards are issued to active, full and part-time employees (some exclusions
apply) on the first day of their birth month. Any unused funds will be removed on
the last day of the same month. These rewards can be redeemed at the following dining
locations: Chick-fil-A, Copperhead Jacks, Eatery, Frogtown Cafe, Market@SU, Paper
Lantern, Subway, and True Burger in the Student Union. Social House in Ottawa East
and Market@OE. Starbucks in Carlson Library. Freshens in North Engineering. Four Seasons
Bistro on Health Science Campus.

**How do I check my balances/usage?**

To check your balances and/or usage from the web, follow the process below:

- Visit rocketcardplus.utoledo.edu
- Enter your UTAD login and password
- On the gray bar, click  on one of these three options: **Account Transactions**, **Board Transactions** or **Account Statements**
- Enter the date range
- Click **Search**

To check your balances from the app, follow the process below:

- Download Transact eAccounts from your digital app store
- Authenticate with your university credentials

#### Rocket Card Issues

**What if my Rocket Card is lost?**

If you lose your Rocket Card (ID) you should freeze your card immediately to protect
your account by following the process below:

- Visit rocketcardplus.utoledo.edu
- Enter your UTAD login and password
- Once logged in, click on **Card Service**
- Click **Freeze Card**

Once the steps above are completed, you will receive an email confirmation in your UToledo email. If your lost card is not recovered, you will need to order a new card, which is subject to a replacement fee. Lost cards that are turned into the ID Office are kept on file for one month, as long as the person is still an active student or faculty/staff member. Please check with the ID Office to see if your lost card has been found. You may also call the Main Campus lost and found at 419.530.2601.

**What if my Rocket Card is worn or torn?**

If your Rocket Card snaps, splits, breaks, or if your image and/or name become unrecognizable,
please visit the Rocket Card Office located in the Student Union room 2525, to have
our ID staff review your replacement options.  Office hours are M-F, 8:30am-4:30pm.
Once our ID staff confirms the replacement should be at no charge, they will walk
you through the reorder steps.

If you work on the Health Science Campus, please send an email to rocketcard@utoledo.edu explaining the situation and include an image of the front and back of your Rocket Card. Our ID staff will review your replacement options with you via email.

**What if my Rocket Card no longer swipes?**

If you are experiencing issues with your Rocket Card, please visit the Rocket Card
Office located in the Student Union room 2525, to have our ID staff troubleshoot your
ID.  The office hours are M-F, 8:30am-4:30pm.  Be sure to keep your Rocket Card away
from magnetic fields, as they may damage the encoding on the card. Do not bend, scratch,
or punch holes in the card.

**What if my Rocket Card displays incorrect information?**

Employees and Affiliates that have a title and/or department change will need to request a new Rocket Card. In order for this replacement to be of no cost, the previous Rocket Card must be surrendered when the updated Rocket Card is picked up.

**How do I order a new or replacement Rocket Card?**

**Students**

- Log into your MyUT
- Click **Request New/Replacement Rocket Card** under **My Other Resources**
- Review personal information
- Select pick-up location
- Upload your photo
- Click **Submit**

**Employees**

- Log into your MyUT
- Click **Request New/Replacement Rocket Card** under **Other Services**
- Review personal information
- Select pick-up location
- Upload your photo
- Click **Submit**

**Affiliates**

- Log into your MyUT
- Click **Request New/Replacement Rocket Card** under **WorkPlace Tools**
- Review personal information
- Select pick-up location
- Upload your photo
- Click **Submit**

**Departmental**

- Visit departmentidupload.utoledo.edu
- Enter your UTAD credentials and follow site instructions

Please note: replacement cards may be subject to a fee. Acceptable forms of payment include cash and checks made payable to The University of Toledo at the time of pick up, no credit cards will be accepted. Rocket IDs will remain available for pick-up for a maximum of 6 months.
