## Food Pantry Eligibility

To be eligible to receive items from the food pantry, all students must be currently enrolled at The University of Toledo.

Students may qualify for additional items if they are experiencing an emergency.

**Examples include:**

- Recent job loss
- Experienced fire, flood or other natural disaster
- Had a change in your household (divorce, death, hospitalization) that has caused financial hardship
- Displaced from residence

Upon your visit, be prepared to provide your **Rocket Number**.
