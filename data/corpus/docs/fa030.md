## Financial Aid Resources

### FINANCIAL AID FOR BEGINNERS

- Types of Financial Aid ― information on types of financial aid to fund your education.
- Financial Aid Basics ― A video from the experts at Rocket Solution Central, your resource for all your financial aid questions.
- Frequently Asked Questions ― answers to some of the most common questions directed to our staff at Rocket Solution Central.
- Understanding Your Financial Aid Offer ― an in-depth description of financial aid awards and processes.
- Federal Student Aid ― a good basic primer on financial aid. Spend some time exploring this site. It describes how the federal government determines if you're eligible for loans, grants and work-study and offers detailed information about all federal student aid.
- Glossary of financial aid terms

### MYUT

- How to access myUT — a video that shows you how to use the student portal to manage your UToledo business (viewing and accepting financial aid; paying bills; checking your academic progress, etc.)

### MONEY MANAGEMENT/FINANCIAL LITERACY

- Financial Literacy connects you to a wide variety of money management tools and tips.
- Rocket Financial Wellness Center ― a great resource of easily digestible online information about how to pay for college and manage money
