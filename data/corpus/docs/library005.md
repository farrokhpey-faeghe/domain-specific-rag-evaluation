## Carlson Library Room Reservation Request

## Reservations

- Carlson Library Room Details
- University Libraries Reservation Guidelines
- Carlson Reservation Space FAQ

Reservations can **only** be made by UToledo Faculty & Staff. Student workers making requests on behalf of
their employer must use supervisor's contact information for acceptance.

**Students** can use the Room Reservation Request form below to reserve CL1024K - Student Interview
Room. Requests made less than 24-hours in advance for CL 1024K may not be considered.

**Student Organizations** must use InvoNet to reserve CL 2003 - Student Organization Suite. Event Request process can be found
on the Office of Student Engagement Event Planning page. Requests made less than a week in advance for CL 2003 will not be considered.

Questions about space, availability, or reservation guidelines may be answered in our Carlson Reservation Space FAQ . Additional questions may be directed to libraryreservations@utoledo.edu.

You will receive notification on the status of your request within **48 business hours.**
