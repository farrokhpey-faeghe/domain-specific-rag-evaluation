## Job Search

### Job Search Guide

Searching for a full-time job or an internship can be both overwhelming and intimidating. A well-crafted job search process takes time, preparation, and concentrated effort. However, it does not need to be a daunting process.

Today’s most successful job seekers utilize various job search strategies to stand
out from the crowd — from establishing a social presence to utilizing job source platforms
to targeting companies. We have created this guide to expedite your job search as
we introduce and steer you through an array of proven resources and methods.

Make an appointment with a career consultant to help you construct a personalized job search strategy. Components of your strategy may include: identifying targeted employers, networking (EPIC Toledo, LinkedIn, professional associations, conferences), searching for jobs online, follow-up timeframes, and interview practice and preparation.

Start your job search by creating a profile on Handshake. Explore resources here and on the MyUT portal to maximize your exposure to opportunities.
