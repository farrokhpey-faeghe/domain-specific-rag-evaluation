## Meal Plans and Campus Dining

Whether you live on campus, commute or work at UToledo, Rocket Dining gives you flexible ways to eat between classes, meetings and campus activities. Compare meal plans, understand meal swipes and Dining Dollars, find dining locations and take the right next step.

#### On-Campus Students

Residents of Honors Academic Village and other on-campus housing must have a meal
plan each semester. **Students living in McComas Village are not required to have a meal plan.**

#### Commuter students

Meal plans are optional for students who live off campus. Choose any of the five student meal plans for a predictable dining budget between classes.

#### Prospective students and families

Review how meal plans work, compare current prices and explore dining locations before enrolling. No university login required.

#### Faculty, staff and affiliates

Employees and affiliates with an active Rocket Number can purchase a reloadable Fuel plan that combines meal swipes with Dining Dollars+.

Every student meal plan combines meal swipes and Dining Dollars. Your plan is assigned to your Rocket Number and works through your Rocket Card or Rocket Mobile ID. There's nothing extra to carry or activate.

#### Meal swipes

Use one swipe for an all-you-care-to-eat visit at The Eatery or for an eligible grab-and-go meal at a participating retail location. Swipes can only be used by the plan holder and expire at the end of each semester.

#### Dining Dollars

A dining balance loaded to your Rocket Card, accepted at dining locations, dining centers and markets across campus. Dining Dollars roll over from fall to spring semester and expire at the end of spring.

#### Guest meal swipes

Every student plan includes five guest meal swipes per semester so you can treat a friend or family member. The current guest swipe location is The Eatery. Guest swipes don't roll over and expire at the end of each semester.

**Dining Dollars+** are optional funds that students, faculty or staff can add to a Rocket Card for extra
flexibility, separate from the Dining Dollars included in a meal plan. Dining Dollars+
is accepted at every Main Campus dining location except Phoenicia Cuisine and 4Seasons
Bistro and follows the same fall-to-spring rollover as Dining Dollars. The minimum
purchase is $25. Purchase Dining Dollars+.

#### How to choose a plan

Choose based on how you expect to eat on campus, not only the plan name.

- How many days a week will you be on campus?
- Will you eat mostly at The Eatery or prefer retail dining and coffee stops?
- Do you want more meal swipes or a larger Dining Dollars balance?
- Will you eat breakfast on campus or expect long class or lab days?

Still not sure? Email mealplan@utoledo.edu before the change deadline. Rocket Dining can explain eligibility and plan features but can't predict your exact usage.

A current student completes this step in myUT with their own UTAD credentials. Parents and family members should never request or use a student's UTAD password.

1. Log in to myUT
2. Under the **My Accounts** section, select **Change or Add Meal Plan**
3. Choose an eligible plan and submit
4. Review the Bursar account for the charge

Residential students are assigned Platinum by default. Once your Bursar bill is available, you may switch to another eligible plan before the published deadline.

#### Meal plans and eligibility

##### Do students living on campus need a meal plan?

Yes. All residents of Honors Academic Village or other on-campus housing are required
to have a meal plan each semester, except students living in **McComas Village**. Review the Dining Agreement for full terms.

##### Can commuter students buy a meal plan?

Yes. Commuter students may choose any of the five student meal plans: Diamond, Platinum, Gold, Silver or Bronze.

##### Which plan is the default for residential students?

Platinum. Residential students with fewer than 60 earned credit hours may switch to Diamond or Gold; students with 60 or more earned hours may also choose Silver or Bronze.

#### Dining Dollars and other balances

##### Do Dining Dollars roll over?

Dining Dollars roll over from fall semester to spring semester and expire at the end of spring semester.

##### What's the difference between Dining Dollars and Dining Dollars+?

Dining Dollars come included with a student meal plan. Dining Dollars+ are optional funds anyone can add to a Rocket Card ($25 minimum), accepted everywhere on Main Campus except Phoenicia Cuisine and 4Seasons Bistro. Both roll over from fall to spring and expire at the end of spring. Meal swipes can't be converted into either one. Purchase Dining Dollars+.

#### Swipes and usage

##### Do meal swipes roll over?

No. Unused meal swipes expire at the end of each semester.

##### Can a student share their Rocket Card or meal swipes?

No. A meal plan and Rocket Card can only be used by the plan holder. Use a guest meal swipe to treat someone else at the designated guest location.

#### Guests and visitor policy

##### What is a guest pass?

"Guest pass" is a common name for a guest meal swipe. Each student plan includes five per semester, and each one lets the plan holder pay for one guest at the designated guest dining location, currently The Eatery. Guest swipes don't roll over.

#### Changing or canceling a plan

##### How can a student change plans?

Use **Change or Add a Meal Plan** under My Accounts in myUT before the published deadline. Additional Dining Dollars+
can be purchased separately online, but meal swipes can't be purchased separately
or added mid-semester.

##### Can a parent purchase or change a student's meal plan?

No. The student completes the transaction in myUT with their own UTAD credentials. Parents and family members can review all the public plan information on this page without logging in.

#### Payment and refunds

##### Where can I ask about cohort pricing or billing?

##### Can I get a refund on unused Dining Dollars or swipes?

No. Meal plan balances are never refunded, rebated or transferred. Cancelling the entire plan is different: a full credit is possible only if you cancel before the first day of classes with no usage; after that, credit is reduced on a daily schedule. Review the Dining Agreement for the full cancellation schedule.

#### Faculty and staff

#### Can I purchase more than one meal plan?

Yes. If you run out of meal swipes, you can purchase another meal plan at any time.

If you only need additional Dining Dollars+, you can purchase them separately.

#### Do Faculty & Staff meal plans expire?

No. Faculty & Staff meal plans do not expire as long as you remain employed or affiliated with the University.

If your employment with the University ends, any remaining meal swipes and Dining Dollars+ become unavailable, unless you are also a currently registered student.

#### How do I use my meal plan at dining kiosks?

At participating dining kiosks, select Meal Swipe on the welcome screen. You will then see the meal swipe options available at that location.

To use Dining Dollars+, select the restaurant option on the welcome screen and place your order. At checkout, choose Campus Card as your payment method, then swipe your Rocket Card or tap your Rocket Mobile ID. Your Dining Dollars+ balance will appear as a payment option.

##### What happens to dining during severe weather?

UT Dining Services may reduce the number of open locations during hazardous weather. Updates are posted right here on this page and shared on Rocket Dining's Facebook, X and Instagram. For campus-wide alerts by text or email, sign up for UT Alert.

##### Is dining open during breaks?

Dining centers close during official University breaks (Thanksgiving and the breaks between fall, winter and spring terms), though some retail locations may stay open with limited hours. Check the Dine On Campus hours page for what's open.
