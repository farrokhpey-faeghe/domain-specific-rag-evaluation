**Updates to how Interlibrary Loan Works!**

**University Libraries migrated to a new system over summer 2025 that simplifies requesting materials not accessible at UToledo! You no longer have to create and log into a separate ILLiad account to request articles or materials unavailable through OhioLINK. Simply visit UTMOST, our new all-in-one library search, log in with your UTAD ID and password, and search for what you need. If you don't find it, there will be options to request it. If your request can't be filled through OhioLINK, it will automatically route to Interlibrary Loan for you.**

Borrowing books through interlibrary loan (ILL) is slightly different than borrowing from University Libraries or through OhioLINK. When you check out an item that was borrowed through ILL, the due date in your library record defaults to 12 weeks with no renewals. (Sometimes, a library will not allow us to have their material that long. In those cases, we will contact you directly ahead of time.)

If you are checking out an ILL item close to the end of the semester, however, your due date will default to the day that your library record expires. If you need additional time with the item you must contact our department at interlibraryloan@utoledo.edu. Renewals are not guaranteed!

Interlibrary Loan offers free access to materials not available in the collections of University Libraries, or through OhioLINK. This service is restricted to affiliated University of Toledo users with library records in good standing (i.e. no overdue materials or outstanding fines).

Please note that our office may refuse to fulfill a request if we determine that it would exceed Fair Use standards, or otherwise violate Copyright law (Title 17, United States Code).

National copyright guidelines annually limit our office to obtain at no charge five articles published in the last five years from each journal not held in University Libraries' collections. Beyond those first five articles, copyright fees (which can exceed $100, depending on the journal) must be paid to the journal publisher. The library strives to fulfill all patron requests, but does not have unlimited resources to fund all fees incurred.

Please consider these factors when placing your requests.

Interlibrary Loan (ILL) requests are processed in the order in which they are received, and there are currently no limits on the number of requests that can be submitted. However, the library reserves the right to limit processing of multiple requests to five per day during busier times. Patrons placing more than five requests should prioritize and submit them in order of importance.

Requests that exceed the daily limit will be held and processed as time permits (at least 5 per day), in the order in which they were received.

If you have further questions, please send email to interlibraryloan@utoledo.edu.
