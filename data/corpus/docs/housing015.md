## Presidents hall

Presidents Hall is a suite-style living residence hall. This building has six person suites with three double bedrooms. Each suite includes a furnished living room and private bathroom.

### Presidents hall Amenities

- Air conditioning
- Single rooms within suites available
- Private bathroom in suite
- Shared study and lounge spaces
- Coin-free laundry
- Work out room
- Computer lab
- Secure Access
- Vending machines
- Co-ed throughout the building
- Outdoor bike storage
- Closest to Rocket Hall, Gateway and the Food Hall at Ottawa East

### Room Specifications

- Bed – XL twin, adjustable low loft
- Closet or wardrobe
- Dresser
- Desk
- Desk chair
- Couches and chairs
- Window blinds
- Ceiling lights

### Honors, Health professions Living Learning Community

This Living Learning Community (LLC) is developed and nurtured through interactive programming, personal engagement and interaction between students, faculty and staff outside the classroom. Engaging team building programs, coupled with exclusive HPLLC events and staff support will help you focus on the team skills you’ll need as a health care professional.
