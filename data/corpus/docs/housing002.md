## Housing Options

- High-rise residence hall exclusively for first year students
- Two-person bedrooms with community bathrooms
- Kitchens, lounges, study spaces, a new computer lab, updated lobby, and brand-new furniture throughout the building
- Closest to the Glass Bowl and the Flatlands

- Houses members of seven sororities and seven fraternities
- Each townhouse contains formal living and dining areas, a full kitchen, laundry room and single, two- and three-person bedrooms
- Residents are members of a Greek chapter and do not have to purchase a meal plan.

- Choice of private or shared accommodations (one-, two- and four-person suites)
- Amenities include a fitness center, computer center with Mac computers, recreation lounge with billiards and study rooms
- Full academic-year leases with all utilities included
