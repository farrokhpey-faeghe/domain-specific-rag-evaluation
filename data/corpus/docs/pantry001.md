## Food Pantries

### Our Mission

We work to provide temporary food aid for enrolled University of Toledo students who
are in need.

Our goals are to:

- Create a sustainable resources to temporarily help meet food needs on campus
- Provide students accessing the food pantries with additional resources to help them succeed
- Involve additional resources and community partners to assure the sustainability of the pantries

### Eligibility

All current UToledo students are eligible to use the food pantries. Please visit our
eligibility page for additional information.
