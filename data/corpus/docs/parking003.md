## Get to Know Your VEO Scooter

### Electric Scooter Sharing

The University of Toledo launched an electric scooter sharing program through a company called VEO. VEO scooters are designed to provide an effortless experience and are a green transportation alternative for students, faculty and staff.

**Please note:**  VEO scooters will be unavailable on campus from mid-November until approximately
mid-March due to cold temperatures and inclement weather.

The battery-powered scooters are located across Main Campus and the Health Science Campus, with users locating and unlocking them through the free VEO app. The app shows real-time scooter locations, making it easy for users to find a scooter throughout campus. After reaching their destination, riders then lock the scooter with the app. VEO scooters and bikes will be deactivated from midnight till 5 am, daily. Every evening, all scooters are collected by VEO fully charged and returned to their original location for use the next day.

Where will you ride first?

- Download the VEO App
- View Nearby Scooters
- Lock Up Safely when finished
