## UToledo Rec Aquatic Center

Welcome to the UToledo Rec Aquatic Center. Our Aquatics Center is home to three unique swimming pools - Lap Pool, Leisure Pool, and Dive Pool. We also offer a relaxing sauna for patron use. Our Lap Pool is perfect for swimming laps and is used for collegiate level competitions. Our Leisure Pool is currently only open for swim lesson use and rentals for birthday parties and other events. Our Dive Pool is reserved for dive groups and events. Come check out the UToledo Rec Aquatics Center to see everything we have to offer.
