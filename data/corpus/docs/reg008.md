## Unofficial Transcripts

### Current and Former Students

**Last attended Main Campus Fall 2000 to present or Health Science Campus Summer 2006
to present.**

If you do not need an official transcript and a listing of your coursework will do, use the my UT portal.

- First, login to the myUT portal using your UTAD credentials. If you are unsure of your UTAD username or password, contact the University's Help Desk at 419.530.2400.
- Locate the Academic Transcripts link on the Student tab, under My Records.
- Click the Academic Transcripts link.
- On the screen, input desired transcript information, click Submit.
- Print a copy.

### Former Students

**Last attended Main Campus Prior to Fall 2000 or Health Science Campus Prior to Summer
2006.**

An **online** unofficial transcript for students who attended prior to fall 2000 is not available.
You may, however, order an official.  Additional information about ordering official transcripts is available on this website.

### Course Descriptions

Use theUT Catalog to look up course descriptions from 1998 to the present.

For catalogs older than 1998 you can submit your request here.

When submitting your course description request you must include the course number, course title and the year the course was taken, (ex: English 101, College Comp. I, Spring of 1985). Also include the mailing address or fax number and attention line of where the descriptions are to be sent. Course descriptions are processed in three business days.

### Contact Information

Email a transcript specialist with questions or comments.
