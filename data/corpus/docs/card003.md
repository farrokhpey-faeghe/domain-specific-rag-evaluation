## Rocket Mobile ID

### **Your Rocket Card…now available in Apple Wallet, Google Wallet, and Samsung Wallet!**

The Rocket Mobile ID is now available to all current students and employees. Tap your iPhone, Apple Watch, Samsung Galaxy, or other eligible Android phones at readers across campus to conveniently use your Rocket Mobile ID. Your Rocket Mobile ID acts just like your physical card and now you can add it to your preferred digital wallet.

### See Rocket Mobile ID in Action

Watch this quick video to learn how to set up your Rocket Mobile ID and use it for everyday activities across campus.

### Getting Started

Minimal setup is required to use your Rocket Mobile ID. Most users can begin using their Rocket Mobile ID on their phone in minutes. An approved photo is required. Once you have requested and received your Rocket Card, that same photo will be displayed on your Rocket Mobile ID.

### To set up your Rocket Mobile ID

1. Ensure you are set up for MultiFactor Authentication.
2. Request a Rocket Card from your myUT portal if you have not already done so.
1. If ordering a new Rocket Card you will need to wait for confirmation that it has been printed before proceeding with the next step.
3. Download the Transact eAccounts app, authenticate with your university credentials
and then add your Rocket Mobile ID to Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or Samsung Wallet.

Please note it may take up to 60 minutes for your Rocket Mobile ID to be fully active.
