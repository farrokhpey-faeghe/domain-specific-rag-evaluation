### Welcome to Education Abroad!

Studying abroad can offer numerous advantages and opportunities for personal and academic growth. We're here to help you with information and guidance on education abroad. Whether you're considering studying in a different country, exploring various programs, or seeking advice on the application process, feel free to ask any questions you may have. Let's get started.

If you aren't sure where to start, we recommend going to our **Get Started page**.

**Dual Application**

You are applying to an international experience through a provider or accredited institution,
which has a dual application process. You must complete an application with:

1. UToledo Education Abroad on the Education Abroad Portal.

**AND**

2. The provider/institution coordinating your program.

NOTE: The provider/institution’s application deadline may differ from UToledo Education
Abroad. It is your responsibility to check their website for up-to-date information
about the program, including their application deadline.

**UToledo Faculty-Led Programs (UToledo-Managed)**

A faculty-led program is an academic initiative where a faculty member organizes and leads a group of students on an educational trip abroad. These programs are designed to offer students a unique learning experience that combines classroom instruction with real-world exposure to the subject matter in an international context.

- Developed by UToledo faculty members.
- You will be with other UToledo students.
- Take UToledo courses that align with the curriculum. *(Some faculty-led programs may not be tied to a course.)*
- Pay UToledo tuition, program fees, and some UToledo fees.

**Exchange Programs**

An exchange program between UToledo and a host institution abroad is an organized partnership designed to foster cultural and academic exchanges. The primary goals include enhancing cross-cultural understanding, improving language skills, and providing participants with a global perspective. Participants will pay tuition and fees at UToledo; however, room and board and other additional fees must be paid directly to the host institution.

- Courses and services are provided by the host university.
- Housing is in shared apartments or dorms.
- Recommended for full immersion—studying alongside local students without direct UToledo oversight.
- Pay UToledo tuition and some UToledo fees. Housing costs are paid directly to the host university.

**Partner (UToledo Approved) Programs**

Third-party providers facilitate enrollment at a host institution or offer their own courses abroad. Students pay program fees (tuition and other fees) to the program provider.

- Managed by third-party providers or universities abroad.
- Housing is arranged by the provider and is mandatory.
- Take classes with U.S., local, and international students.
- Apply to and pay the program provider directly. This includes tuition, housing, on-site support, excursions, and sometimes meals.

For a comprehensive list of study abroad programs, please visit our UToledo Approved Programs page.

### My Experience

### Sebastian Shaskus: Finance (minor in International Business and Spanish)

#### Uruguay, Spring 2023

“If you’re planning on studying abroad, definitely do it! It can certainly be scary at times and if you're going to a country with a vastly different language and culture the culture shock may get you the first few weeks, but I promise after you get through settling in, you're due for the experience of a lifetime. You'll also meet some great and likeminded people and make lifelong friends and memories. Also, financial aid is very generous and in total the whole thing cost about the same as a semester at UToledo. UToledo and each specific program also offer scholarships as well!”

### Jordyn Fearon: Pre-law

#### England, Summer 2023

“I always had a dream to study abroad because I thought it would be such a fun and exciting experience. Even if you're considering it just do it. Living in a new country is such a fun and life-changing experience”

### Karsten Gaffne: German

#### Germany, Fall 2022

“My biggest piece of advice for students wanting to study abroad: just take the leap. Take any opportunity you can while abroad because you never know what you'll get to experience. It might sound scary, but you'll never know if you don't try. Your friends want to take a 7-hour train ride to Poland for thanksgiving break and they invite you? Go with them! There's a concert going on tomorrow night at a club in town? Dress up and have fun! There's a cute guy in your study abroad group? Ask him to go on a date with you in Hamburg! You never know where your study abroad trip will take you, during and even after your life abroad.”

### Scott Sobnosky: Public Health (minor in Chemistry)

“Do it! It enriched my life with many unique adventures and experiences I wouldn't have been exposed to if I didn't study abroad. Start getting information about study abroad now if you are interested! The planning process is thorough and takes a while so the sooner you start the better!”
